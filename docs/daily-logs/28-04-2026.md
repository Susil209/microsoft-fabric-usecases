## Risk scoring calibration issue found

**Problem:** 79.8% of patients flagged HIGH at threshold=35%.
Root cause: score distribution clusters between 14–85 with median 46.7.
A threshold of 35% sits below the median → majority class flagged.

**Fix applied:** Switched to percentile-based thresholds.

- HIGH   = top 20% of scores (≥ p80)
- MEDIUM = p50 to p80
- LOW    = bottom 50% (< p50)
