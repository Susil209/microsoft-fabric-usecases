## Baseline results

- The final modeling dataset included 71,518 patient records after cleaning and deduplication, with 3,224 positive 30-day readmissions and a positive rate of about 4.5 percent.

- The class imbalance was handled using scale_pos_weight of about 21.2 in XGBoost, based on the negative-to-positive class ratio.
​

- Five-fold stratified cross-validation produced a mean AUC-ROC of 0.6433 with a standard deviation of 0.0074.
​

- The final train/test experiment achieved a holdout AUC-ROC of 0.6647, average precision of 0.0942, and best iteration of 32 with early stopping.
​

- SHAP importance indicated that the strongest features included number_inpatient, ageord, numlabprocedures, priorvisitstotal, numberdiagnoses, timeinhospital, and nummedications.

## Interpretation

- The current baseline is a reasonable first result for a difficult and highly imbalanced hospital readmission problem, especially because only about 4.5 percent of records belong to the positive class.

- The model is learning clinically sensible signals: prior inpatient usage, age, intensity of lab testing, diagnostic burden, and hospital stay length are all strong indicators of future readmission risk.

- The holdout AUC-ROC of 0.6647 is better than random ranking and consistent with the notebook’s own assessment that this is an acceptable starting point with room for improvement.

## Issues faced

- The main issue occurred during the initial CSV-to-table load because Delta Lake does not allow spaces or some special characters in column names. This was resolved by renaming the raw headers before saving the bronze table. 

- The dataset also contained substantial missingness in fields such as weight, medicalspecialty, and payercode, which required explicit cleaning decisions before modeling. weight was dropped, while other high-null fields were retained for possible future use.

- The problem is strongly imbalanced, with only 3,224 positives out of 71,518 records, so performance metrics needed to focus on AUC-ROC and average precision rather than raw accuracy.

- The current baseline is still moderate in predictive power, which means additional tuning, calibration, and feature improvements will be needed before calling the solution production-ready.
